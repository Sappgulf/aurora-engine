# Product North Star

Aurora is the best compact Rust engine for deterministic, campaign-driven 2D
strategy games that run natively and instantly in browsers, with agent-native
playtesting built into the engine.

Aurora: Last Light proves that identity through original systems: power as
territorial connectivity, persistent named specialists, intelligence as a
combat resource, transforming environments, and campaign decisions that alter
simulation rules.

Success means deep strategic clarity and reproducible behavior, not raw feature
count and not imitation of another game's content.
